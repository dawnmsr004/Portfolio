02 / Featured project · Leonardo Hotel

A city in full sail.

Environmental storytelling inspired by Aberdeen's Tall Ships Races, 2025.

Aberdeen · 2025

Turning a window
into a destination.

Sailing ships painted across the glass, with layered brushwork and richly varied colours.
Sunlight creates an additional composition: the painted sails cast striking ship-shaped shadows across the wall.
Daylight study — the painted ship creates a second image through its shadow.
Evening study — shifting ambient light changes the colours and atmosphere of the window installation.

From local cultural moment
to guest-facing artwork.

For the 2025 Tall Ships Races in Aberdeen, I independently conceived, illustrated and painted a large-scale maritime installation across the windows of Leonardo Hotel, opposite the railway station. The artwork brought the city's nautical celebrations into the hotel environment through ships, a lighthouse and sweeping waves. I deliberately designed the sails and layered colours to interact with natural light: in direct sun, the translucent paint shifts in appearance and the sails project a second, shadow-made image onto the surrounding wall. At dusk, the same installation reads differently against the illuminated interior. This changing relationship between sunlight, colour and shadow was part of the original concept. My role: concept, illustration and on-site painting.

03 / Café Harmony · Window art

Hand-drawn botanical and wine illustrations created directly on the restaurant's windows.

Brand personality,

At Café Harmony, I created original storefront illustrations combining botanical motifs, wine bottles, glasses and expressive lettering.
